The Problem

Compliance and trust live on different islands.
They shouldn't.

Carriers manage filings on government portals. Brokers vet carriers on Carrier411 and RMIS. Shippers run separate audits on top of all of it. Every party pays for partial information and still gets burned by the gaps between systems.

The current state

Five tools. Three roles. No source of truth.

  • Carriers juggle FMCSA, IRS, UCR, IRP, IFTA, and 50 state portals
  • Brokers pay for Carrier411, MyCarrierPackets, RMIS, Highway separately
  • Shippers run yet another audit before tendering a load
  • Double-brokering scams cost the industry billions per year
  • A carrier's expired insurance is found out after the claim, not before
  • Nobody trusts anyone's data because everyone has different data
With FleetAxis

One axis. One source of truth. Three roles.

  • Every credential, filing, and renewal in a single vault
  • Real-time verification of any USDOT, MC, or broker bond
  • Live insurance certificates synced from underwriters
  • Double-brokering and identity-fraud detection built in
  • Two-way trust scores visible to both sides of every load
  • Audit-ready records the moment freight is questioned
